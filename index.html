About the programme

Dedicated to water. Built for every stage.

  • The Business Innovation Hub is an entrepreneurship and MSME support programme dedicated entirely to water, supporting innovators at multiple stages of development, from pre-revenue to scale-up.
  • It is part of the Joint Cooperation in Applied Research (JCAR) on Water, a bilateral cooperation programme between the Netherlands and Egypt.
  • The Hub is delivered in partnership with the Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation (MWRI), which defines the priority challenges the Hub addresses.
  • It supports Egypt's National Water Resources Plan and the transition to Irrigation System 2.0.
  • The Hub is demand-driven: solutions must respond to real MWRI challenges.

Solutions can be technologies, innovative services, or operational models.

Challenge themes

Four priority areas, defined with MWRI

Your solution should address a real challenge in one or more of these themes. The examples are illustrative; other ideas within each theme are welcome.

THEME A

Smart monitoring and automation

Real-time monitoring, sensors, drones, satellite-based water accounting, leak detection, AI predictive maintenance, SCADA.

THEME B

Nature-based solutions

Nature-based canal rehabilitation and bank protection, beneficial use of Nile sediment, Delta coastal protection, biological control of aquatic weeds and water hyacinth.

THEME C

Clean water: drainage and pollutant management

Clearing and recycling weeds, debris and sediment, water hyacinth valorisation, affordable drainage-water treatment for reuse, waste removal at pumping-station intakes.

THEME D

Renewable-energy transition

Solar or hybrid energy for pump stations, renewable-powered smart groundwater pumping, low-emission pump-station designs.

Support tiers

Support that matches your stage

One programme, three stages: from pre-revenue innovators to scale-ups ready to deploy.

Selected innovators receive grant funding plus technical and business support.
Tier 1

Concept

For pre-revenue innovators: start-ups, research teams and students developing a water solution.

  • Grant support
  • Intensive incubation programme in Egypt
  • Business and financial foundations
  • Technical coaching to get your solution ready for piloting
Tier 2

Piloting

For solutions shown to work at least once and ready to pilot under real conditions.

  • Grant support
  • Targeted technical and business coaching
  • Pilot co-design with MWRI
  • Field-testing support
Tier 3

Scale-up

For proven solutions from established enterprises, ready to scale and deploy.

  • Grant support for scale-up
  • Applicant co-contribution required
Not sure which tier fits you?
  1. Are you pre-revenue and still developing or validating your solution? Choose Tier 1 – Concept.
  2. Has your solution worked at least once, and is it ready for a real-world pilot? Choose Tier 2 – Piloting.
  3. Are you an established enterprise with a proven solution ready to deploy at scale? Choose Tier 3 – Scale-up.
